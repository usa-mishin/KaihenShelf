# FAQ

## Does it work on macOS?

The app currently supports Windows only. The English website does not mean the app UI is translated; the app currently uses Japanese.

## Is BOOTH Library Manager required?

It is needed for product information integration. You can also register local folders without it. KaihenShelf does not download purchased product files.

## The Add button does not open my package manager

Copy the repository URL from the installation guide and paste it into VCC or ALCOM's repository settings. Then install KaihenShelf in the intended project.

## Images are missing after reconnecting to the internet

Click **データ再取得** (Reload data), or reopen the window to retry. Images cannot be shown if their original data or URLs are no longer available.

## Avatar detection is incorrect

Use **情報を編集** (Edit information) to adjust avatar tags and compatibility. Automatic detection is based on the available product and purchase information.

## Does uninstalling delete my imported assets?

Removing the tool package does not remove imported products. Settings and history are stored outside the package. See the [data policy](../policy) for their locations.

## How do I report a problem?

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/usa-mishin/KaihenShelf/issues) with your Unity and KaihenShelf versions, steps to reproduce, and error text. Do not attach purchased files, personal purchase data, or your database.

For a private inquiry, contact [Usatrot Works](https://usatrot-works.booth.pm/) or [email us](mailto:usamishin.art@gmail.com).
